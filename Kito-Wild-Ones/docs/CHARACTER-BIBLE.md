# Character Bible — Kito & the Wild Ones

**Status:** Production specifications (Phase 2). No final character artwork yet.  
**Tagline:** Big Adventures. Little Lessons.  
**Audience:** Ages ~4–8  
**World:** Sunrise Valley  

**Original IP rule:** All characters are original. Do not copy or closely imitate existing cartoon franchises, trademarked designs, or copyrighted characters.

This bible is the source of truth for personality, design direction, voice, motion, and consistency. The human creator remains the final creative decision-maker.

## Kito

### Age / species
- **Age:** About 7 (child-audience equivalent)
- **Species:** Human child
- **Role:** Curious lead explorer of Sunrise Valley; the “&” in *Kito & the Wild Ones*

### Core personality
Warm, brave-curious, and open-hearted. Kito treats every day like an invitation to discover something wonderful — then shares it with friends.

### Secondary personality traits
- Optimistic storyteller (“What if we…?”)
- Quick to apologize and repair friendships
- Slightly dramatic when excited (big gestures, bigger ideas)
- Soft-spoken when someone is sad; loud when celebrating

### Strengths
- Natural leader without bossiness
- Notices details others miss (sounds, tracks, colors in the sky)
- Brings the group back together after disagreements
- Turns mistakes into learning moments without shame

### Weaknesses
- Impatience — rushes ahead before the plan is ready
- Over-confidence in new places
- Can forget to ask for help until stuck
- Sometimes talks over quieter friends when excited

### Character motivation
To explore Sunrise Valley safely and joyfully, and to make sure nobody feels left out of the adventure.

### Typical expressions
- Wide bright eyes + open smile (discovery)
- Hand-to-chin thinking face
- Determined brows with a forward lean
- Soft concern when a friend is upset
- Triumphant “we did it” grin shared with the group

### Body proportions
- Child proportions: large head (~1/4–1/5 of body height), shorter limbs
- Slightly taller than Bobo; shorter than Zuri’s neck-line and Mama Tembo
- Soft rounded joints; readable hands (4 fingers + thumb stylized OK if consistent)

### General silhouette
- Compact upright explorer
- Distinctive short curly/coily hair volume readable in silhouette
- Light adventure satchel or sash that reads at thumbnail size
- Clear “kid hero” outline — never bulky, never spindly

### Clothing direction
- Everyday: soft earth-tone shorts, warm-toned short-sleeve shirt with a simple original geometric motif (not copied from any brand)
- Optional light sash or small satchel for explorer identity
- Barefoot or simple soft sandals (series rule: pick one and keep consistent)
- No logos, no franchise references, no adult fashion trends

### Color direction
- Skin: rich warm brown with healthy sunrise highlights
- Outfit: terracotta / soft gold / leafy green accents (must stay distinct from Zuri’s stripes and Jabari’s gray)
- Hair: deep dark brown / near-black with soft rim light in sunrise scenes

### Voice personality
- Bright child voice; clear diction for young listeners
- Curious upward inflection on questions
- Warm laugh; never sarcastic or mean
- Short sentences; simple vocabulary
- Avoid catchphrases that sound like other shows

### Movement personality
- Energetic but controlled bounce-walk
- Leans forward when investigating
- Hands talk: pointing, waving friends over, gentle shoulder taps
- Freeze-and-listen beats before big discoveries

### Character relationships
- **Zuri:** Trusts Zuri’s ideas; often asks “What do you think?”
- **Bobo:** Laughs with Bobo; gently steers Bobo when play gets too wild
- **Jabari:** Relies on Jabari for courage and “we can do this” moments
- **Mama Tembo:** Looks up to Mama Tembo; seeks comfort and wisdom after hard lessons

### Things the character should NEVER do
- Bully, mock, or exclude anyone
- Use dangerous “dare” behavior presented as cool
- Disrespect Mama Tembo or elders
- Solve problems with cruelty or trickery that hurts others
- Break the world’s safety tone (no real peril framed as fun)

### Animation consistency rules
- Keep hair volume, satchel/sash, and outfit colors locked after approval
- Eyes stay large and readable; pupils never tiny or uncanny
- Emotion first in face + posture before fancy motion
- Same walk cycle personality across episodes (curious bounce, not adult stride)

## Zuri

### Age / species
- **Age:** Young kid-equivalent (roughly peer to Kito)
- **Species:** Young zebra (anthropomorphic, bipedal-friendly for acting; may drop to four legs for play/run if designed)
- **Role:** Clever, caring friend; the group’s thoughtful problem-framer

### Core personality
Bright, kind, and quietly confident. Zuri notices patterns — in nature and in feelings — and helps the Wild Ones choose the kinder path.

### Secondary personality traits
- Gentle humor (witty, never cutting)
- Patient teacher energy with younger-feeling moments
- Loves sorting, mapping, and “let’s make a plan”
- Sensitive to unfairness

### Strengths
- Strong listener
- Excellent memory for places and promises
- Creative problem-solving with simple tools/props
- Calms the group when energy spikes

### Weaknesses
- Overthinking — can freeze when options multiply
- Hesitant to speak up until invited (early-series arc)
- Holds onto worry longer than Kito
- Perfectionism about “doing it right”

### Character motivation
To keep friends safe and harmonious, and to prove that smart + kind can lead the way.

### Typical expressions
- Soft smile with attentive eyes
- One-ear tilt (listening / curiosity)
- Brow furrow + gentle smirk when solving a puzzle
- Warm eye-crinkle reassurance
- Rare full laugh that lights the whole face

### Body proportions
- Longer neck than other kids (readable zebra identity) but not so long it becomes slapstick every shot
- Elegant but soft kid proportions — not adult fashion-model zebra
- Legs longer than Kito’s; torso compact

### General silhouette
- Graceful vertical line with striped neck/body read
- Mane as a short soft crest (original shape — not copied from any franchise)
- Distinctive ear shapes readable in profile and front

### Clothing direction
- Minimal clothing that respects animal body: woven scarf-sash, bead-like accent necklace (original patterns), or light wrap
- Patterns inspired by contemporary African textile geometry — abstracted, original, never sacred-symbol misuse
- Keep clothing secondary to stripes so silhouette stays zebra-first

### Color direction
- Classic high-contrast black/white stripes with soft warm-gray midtones (avoid pure harsh digital black)
- Soft cream muzzle; warm brown eyes
- Scarf/sash accent: sunrise coral or deep teal (pick one series accent and lock it)

### Voice personality
- Clear, thoughtful mid-child voice
- Measured pacing; kind tone
- Asks clarifying questions
- Never snobby “smart kid” arrogance

### Movement personality
- Smooth, intentional steps
- Neck and head lead emotional acting
- Small precise gestures when explaining
- Faster hoof-taps when excited or nervous

### Character relationships
- **Kito:** Best-idea partner; balances Kito’s rush with planning
- **Bobo:** Affectionate eye-rolls; protects Bobo’s feelings when jokes go wrong
- **Jabari:** Mutual respect; Zuri supplies strategy, Jabari supplies steadiness
- **Mama Tembo:** Student of patience; often the first to seek Mama Tembo’s advice

### Things the character should NEVER do
- Belittle others for being “less smart”
- Use intelligence to manipulate
- Abandon the group to be right alone
- Become a pure exposition machine (still a kid, still playful)

### Animation consistency rules
- Stripe pattern must be stable (document a stripe map once designed)
- Neck length constant across shots
- Accent cloth color locked
- Cleverness shown through acting, not constant lecturing poses

## Bobo

### Age / species
- **Age:** Young kid-equivalent (slightly more impulsive peer)
- **Species:** Young rock hyrax (anthropomorphic)
- **Role:** Playful, energetic companion; comic spark and emotional icebreaker

### Core personality
Joy in motion. Bobo turns ordinary moments into games, hugs, and accidental discoveries.

### Secondary personality traits
- Loyal hugger
- Easily distracted by snacks, shiny rocks, funny sounds
- Brave in short bursts; scared of “maybe-monsters” that turn out silly
- Surprisingly insightful one-liners after chaos

### Strengths
- Raises group morale instantly
- Physical comedy that stays gentle and age-safe
- Finds unexpected solutions by “playing” with objects
- Honest about feelings (“I’m scared” / “I’m sorry”)

### Weaknesses
- Impulse control
- Interrupts plans
- Mess-maker (well-intentioned)
- Avoids hard conversations by joking

### Character motivation
To have fun with friends every day — and to be loved even when silly.

### Typical expressions
- Huge open-mouth grin
- Squinty laugh eyes
- “Oops” face (raised brows, tiny frown)
- Puffed cheeks when concentrating
- Dramatic fake-fear that melts into relief giggles

### Body proportions
- Smallest of the kid cast
- Round body, short limbs, big expressive face
- Soft belly read; sturdy little paws
- Ears and whisker-pads as key facial anchors

### General silhouette
- Compact potato-meets-hero shape
- Round ears; short stubby tail optional
- Reads as “small and huggable” at thumbnail size

### Clothing direction
- Simple wrap-belt or colorful shorts-panel with original pattern
- Optional tiny backpack or seed-pouch (comic prop magnet)
- Avoid over-costuming; Bobo’s shape is the joke engine

### Color direction
- Warm brown / taupe fur with soft cream chest
- Nose and paw pads: dusty rose-brown
- Clothing accent: sunny yellow-gold (distinct from Kito’s terracotta and Zuri’s sash)

### Voice personality
- Higher, bubbly kid voice
- Quick tempo; playful sound effects between words (original only)
- Giggles as punctuation
- Never mean-spirited teasing

### Movement personality
- Bouncy hops, rolls, side-steps
- Tackles friends with gentle hug-launches
- Freeze-frame “statue” bits when trying to be sneaky (fails cutely)
- Fast arms, slower brain — then sudden clever beat

### Character relationships
- **Kito:** Looks up to Kito; copies explorer poses
- **Zuri:** Seeks Zuri’s approval after messes
- **Jabari:** Climbing/play partner; hides behind Jabari when startled
- **Mama Tembo:** Instant comfort zone; Mama Tembo’s trunk hugs reset Bobo

### Things the character should NEVER do
- Cruel pranks
- Dangerous stunts treated as comedy
- Steal focus by mocking someone’s feelings
- Be the “dumb friend” stereotype — Bobo is playful, not incapable

### Animation consistency rules
- Stay smallest scale vs peers
- Round silhouette preserved (no sudden skinny redesigns)
- Comedy timing: anticipation → soft impact → recover
- Yellow accent locked; fur value range locked

## Jabari

### Age / species
- **Age:** Young kid-equivalent (peer)
- **Species:** Young black rhinoceros calf (anthropomorphic)
- **Role:** Brave, loyal friend; steady heart of the Wild Ones

### Core personality
Courage expressed as kindness and reliability. Jabari stands firm for friends — then invites them to take the next step together.

### Secondary personality traits
- Quiet pride in helping
- Protective without being controlling
- Loves practice, training games, and “try again”
- Soft shy smile when praised

### Strengths
- Emotional bravery (admits fear, still helps)
- Strong and careful with that strength
- Dependable follow-through
- Grounding presence in panic moments

### Weaknesses
- Stubborn once decided
- Hard on himself after mistakes
- Slow to change a plan mid-stream
- Can mistake asking for help as “not brave”

### Character motivation
To protect the people he loves and to become braver in honest, kid-sized ways.

### Typical expressions
- Steady eyes + small confident smile
- Determined jaw set (not angry)
- Embarrassed hoof-scuff when complimented
- Soft worry brows when friends argue
- Warm triumph shared, never boastful

### Body proportions
- Stockier than Kito/Zuri; solid kid-rhino frame
- Large head with small developing horn (rounded, soft, child-safe design — never sharp weaponized)
- Strong legs; shorter neck than Zuri
- Weight reads in footsteps

### General silhouette
- Broad, sturdy, friendly tank-shape softened for kids
- Horn as signature but gentle
- Ears and horn create unique head profile

### Clothing direction
- Minimal: woven chest band / arm wrap with original geometric motif
- Earth pigments and bead-like accents (tasteful, modern, non-sacred misuse)
- Clothing must not hide the rhino body read

### Color direction
- Cool warm-gray hide with soft lavender-brown shadows (premium, not muddy)
- Subtle lighter belly
- Accent wrap: deep indigo or clay red (lock one; keep distinct from others)
- Eyes: warm brown

### Voice personality
- Lower kid voice than Bobo/Kito, still clearly a child
- Measured, sincere, few words with weight
- Encouraging phrases; no tough-guy aggression
- Softens for emotional scenes

### Movement personality
- Planted stance; deliberate steps
- Shoulder-led acting
- Slow powerful gestures; sudden speed only for rescue/play bursts
- Kneels/lowers to Bobo’s eye level often

### Character relationships
- **Kito:** Loyal second; challenges Kito when rushing into risk
- **Zuri:** Trusts Zuri’s plans; provides the “I’ve got you” muscle
- **Bobo:** Patient big-sib energy; play-wrestles gently
- **Mama Tembo:** Deep respect; models Mama Tembo’s calm strength

### Things the character should NEVER do
- Bully with size or strength
- Glorify fighting
- Shame others for fear
- Use the horn as a violent weapon (playful accidental bonks only, soft and rare)

### Animation consistency rules
- Horn size/shape locked (child-safe roundness)
- Mass and footfall weight consistent
- Bravery shown through care, not dominance poses
- Accent wrap color locked

## Mama Tembo

### Age / species
- **Age:** Adult (parent/mentor generation)
- **Species:** Adult African elephant (anthropomorphic-friendly acting; may remain more quadrupedal with expressive trunk/face)
- **Role:** Wise, grounding adult guide of Sunrise Valley

### Core personality
Gentle authority. Mama Tembo leads with warmth, humor, and patience — teaching through stories and presence more than lectures.

### Secondary personality traits
- Dry playful wit
- Deep memory of valley seasons and friendships
- Protective guardian instinct
- Celebrates children’s bravery loudly and kindly

### Strengths
- Emotional regulation model for the cast
- Conflict mediation
- Practical wisdom (nature, safety, community)
- Makes every child feel seen

### Weaknesses
- Can be overly cautious when kids need a stretch challenge
- Carries quiet worry about the children’s safety
- Sometimes answers with a story when kids want a yes/no (soft comedy)
- Hard time saying goodbye / ending playtime

### Character motivation
To raise courageous, kind children who love their home and each other.

### Typical expressions
- Slow blinking calm eyes
- Trunk curl smile equivalent
- Knowing raised brow
- Wide delighted eyes for children’s successes
- Soft solemn face for serious lessons (still safe, never scary)

### Body proportions
- Largest character; towering but soft presence
- Big rounded body; strong pillar legs
- Expressive trunk as primary acting tool
- Ears large and emotionally readable

### General silhouette
- Iconic elephant mountain of comfort
- Ears + trunk + dome head readable at any distance
- Feels like a moving piece of the landscape — stable, beloved

### Clothing direction
- Elegant minimal adornment: original patterned cloth drape, headband, or beaded trunk cuff
- Colors richer/deeper than the kids’ accents (adult palette)
- Cultural inspiration modern and respectful — no costume-stereotype shortcuts
- Never overdecorated; dignity through simplicity

### Color direction
- Soft charcoal / warm slate elephant gray with rose-brown undertones in sunrise light
- Cloth: deep teal, gold thread accents, or terracotta geometry (locked set)
- Eyes: dark warm brown with kind catchlights

### Voice personality
- Warm adult voice; low and soothing
- Clear storytelling cadence
- Affectionate nicknames for the Wild Ones (original only)
- Never harsh yelling; firmness through calm tone

### Movement personality
- Slow, grounded, intentional
- Trunk gestures for pointing, hugging, lifting gently, comic flourishes
- Kneels/lowers to child eye-lines often
- Stillness is powerful — holds poses longer than kids

### Character relationships
- **Kito:** Mentor and safe harbor; encourages leadership with humility
- **Zuri:** Nurtures Zuri’s confidence to speak
- **Bobo:** Infinite patience; comic foil for Bobo’s chaos
- **Jabari:** Kindred steady spirits; teaches soft strength

### Things the character should NEVER do
- Humiliate children as “discipline”
- Deliver scary moral panic
- Solve every plot instantly (kids must still act)
- Become absent/neglectful without story care
- Be portrayed as magical-stereotype “mystic” instead of a real caring adult

### Animation consistency rules
- Scale relationship to kids locked (Mama Tembo always clearly adult-large)
- Trunk acting language documented and reused
- Cloth pattern locked
- Calm timing: fewer frames of frantic motion; more held emotion

## Series-wide animation consistency rules

1. Silhouettes must remain identifiable in monochrome thumbnail tests.
2. Each character keeps a unique accent color that does not collide with others.
3. No redesign mid-episode without creator approval.
4. Emotions lead animation; dialogue never fights the face.
5. Comedy is gentle; peril is mild; endings restore warmth.
6. Original designs only — no franchise lookalikes.
7. Document approved turnarounds in each `characters/<name>/` folder when artwork begins.
8. Human creator final-approves any bible change that affects locked design.

---

## Consistency checklist (before locking artwork)

1. Silhouette readable at small thumbnail size  
2. Colors distinct across the main cast  
3. Outfit / accent rules written and followed  
4. Voice direction matches age-appropriate tone  
5. No resemblance to copyrighted franchise characters  
6. Relationships and “never do” rules reflected in scripts  
7. Human creator has given final approval

# Visual Bible — Sunrise Valley

**Series:** Kito & the Wild Ones  
**Tagline:** Big Adventures. Little Lessons.  
**Status:** Phase 2 visual direction (no final production artwork yet)  
**Goal:** A warm, cinematic, playful, African-inspired, modern, premium, child-friendly, memorable look — not generic AI-cartoon aesthetics.

---

## Creative north star

Sunrise Valley should feel like **golden morning after rain**: hopeful light, living landscapes, handmade cultural warmth, and premium clarity. Every frame should be readable to a 4–8 year old and beautiful to a parent watching beside them.

**Feel words:** warm · cinematic · playful · African · modern · premium · child-friendly · memorable

**Avoid:**
- Flat purple-on-white / purple-to-indigo default AI themes
- Generic “plastic toy” CGI with empty eyes
- Over-sharpened hyper-detail noise
- Cluttered sticker-bomb composition
- Stereotype costumes or sacred-symbol misuse
- Dark horror contrast, grim desaturation, or chaotic flashy cuts

---

## Environment design

Sunrise Valley is a welcoming highland-savanna valley where community and nature share the same stage.

### Core biomes (reusable kits)
1. **Sunrise Ridge** — open grass hills, long light, lookout rocks  
2. **Acacia Shade Groves** — dappled light, play spaces, story circles  
3. **River Ribbon** — shallow sparkling water, stepping stones, reflections  
4. **Home Clearing** — Mama Tembo’s gathering place, soft paths, communal shade  
5. **Berry Slope / Little Market Nook** — gentle commerce/play props, woven baskets, colorful cloth accents  

Environments are **modular kits**, not one-off paintings every episode.

### Design rules
- Clear foreground / midground / background stages
- Big readable shapes first; detail second
- Always a path kids can “enter” visually
- Nature feels alive (grass sway, leaf flicker) but never overstimulating

---

## Architecture

Architecture is inspired by contemporary and vernacular African building languages — rounded forms, earthen materials, woven textures, shaded courtyards — **modernized and original**.

### Principles
- Soft adobe / rammed-earth volumes with curved corners
- Thatched or gently sloping roofs; deep eaves for shade storytelling
- Open verandas and story-circle clearings
- Handcrafted wood posts, carved (original) geometric panels
- Cloth canopies in sunrise coral, teal, and gold for color punctuation

### Do / Don’t
- **Do:** cozy scale, community-first spaces, shade as a design feature  
- **Don’t:** copy real sacred sites, brand architecture, or tourist-cliché “theme park Africa”

---

## Landscape

- Rolling hills with soft silhouettes against a luminous sky
- Acacia and mixed indigenous tree shapes (stylized, original)
- Warm soil paths with packed-earth texture
- Occasional baobab-like landmark tree as a series “friend location” (original design)
- Distant blue-lavender mountains for depth (never stormy-threatening by default)
- Flora color accents: wildflowers in coral, marigold, soft violet (sparingly)

Landscape should feel **exploratory but safe**.

---

## Character proportions (series)

- **Kids (Kito, Zuri, Bobo, Jabari):** stylized child proportions; large heads; expressive faces; soft hands/paws
- **Mama Tembo:** clearly adult scale; comforting mass; trunk/ears as acting tools
- Maintain consistent relative scale chart across episodes (lock a scale lineup sheet when art begins)

Readable silhouettes > anatomical realism.

---

## Lighting

Lighting is a main character.

### Default key
- **Golden sunrise key light** from low angle (series signature)
- Soft warm fill; gentle cool bounce from sky
- Cream-gold rim light separating characters from backgrounds

### Emotional lighting variants
- **Playful noon:** brighter, cleaner, still warm (not harsh white)
- **Storytime dusk:** honey amber + soft indigo shadows (safe, never scary)
- **Tender scenes:** lower contrast, softer diffusion, closer key

No pitch-black night episodes unless creator explicitly requests and safety tone is preserved.

---

## Time of day

**Primary series time:** early morning to late morning (“sunrise window”).  
This supports the brand promise and keeps color science consistent.

Secondary allowed times (special episodes):
- Golden afternoon
- Soft dusk story circles

Avoid frequent random time jumps that break palette memory.

---

## Camera style

- **Lens feel:** mild cinematic (equivalent of ~35–50mm story; occasional wider establishing)
- **Height:** often near child eye-level; Mama Tembo shots may start low then tilt up with respect
- **Stability:** smooth, motivated moves; no shaky-cam
- **Clarity:** faces and emotional beats always readable

---

## Cinematography

### Shot vocabulary
- Wide establishing of Sunrise Valley (beauty + geography)
- Mediums for group problem-solving
- Close-ups for feelings and lessons
- Gentle insert shots (hands, footprints, river sparkle) for rhythm

### Movement vocabulary
- Slow push-ins for wonder and emotional realization
- Lateral tracking with walks
- Parallax multiplane on backgrounds for premium depth
- Rare playful whip-pans only for Bobo comedy — used sparingly

### Editing rhythm
- Patient enough for ages 4–8
- One idea per shot whenever possible
- Hold reactions after jokes and lessons

## Color philosophy

### Series palette pillars
| Role | Direction |
|------|-----------|
| Light | Warm gold / apricot sunrise |
| Land | Soft savanna greens + clay earth |
| Sky | Clear aqua transitioning to soft peach at horizon |
| Accents | Coral, teal, marigold (controlled) |
| Neutrals | Warm grays, sand, charcoal (Mama Tembo / rocks) |

### Rules
- Characters own distinct accent colors (see Character Bible)
- Backgrounds support, never fight, character read
- Saturation rich but not neon
- Premium look = harmonious restraint, not gray dullness

---

## Texture philosophy

- **Suggest materials**, don’t over-render pores and noise
- Cloth: soft weave visible at medium distance
- Earth: gentle grain; paths feel walkable
- Fur/hide: stylized clumps or soft gradient locks — consistent per character
- Avoid noisy AI “detail soup”

Texture should feel **hand-crafted and intentional**.

---

## Clothing philosophy

- Functional, playful, culturally inspired, modern
- Original geometric motifs influenced by African textile traditions — abstracted and respectful
- Kids: lighter, simpler, mobile
- Mama Tembo: deeper colors, dignified minimal adornment
- No logos, no franchise echoes, no sacred-symbol cosplay

Clothing is identity punctuation, not costume overload.

---

## African cultural inspiration

Inspiration is **celebratory, contemporary, and specific to Sunrise Valley as fiction**, not a documentary of one real place.

### Draw from (as creative influence, not copy)
- Warm communal outdoor living
- Pattern language from textile geometry (originalized)
- Music of light and shade in courtyard/veranda spaces
- Respect for elders and storytelling circles
- Joyful color as everyday beauty

### Guardrails
- No caricature, no colonial tropes, no “exotic peril” framing
- Characters are individuals, not symbols
- Creator approval required for any cultural motif that could be sensitive

---

## Props

Props should be reusable, story-clear, and tactile.

### Prop classes
- Explorer tools (Kito’s satchel items: map scrap, shiny stone, whistle — original designs)
- Play objects (seed drums, vine jump-ropes, rolling fruit)
- Home objects (woven baskets, water calabash forms, story mats)
- Nature tokens (feathers, smooth river stones, flower crowns)

### Rules
- One hero prop per beat
- Big silhouettes; limited tiny clutter
- Store approved props in `props/`

---

## Weather

Default weather: **clear sunrise with soft breeze**.

Allowed gentle variants:
- Cotton-ball clouds
- Light warm rain that ends in rainbow (rare special)
- Misty morning for mystery-soft episodes (still friendly)

Avoid: violent storms, scary thunder as threat engine, extreme heat despair.

Weather supports mood; it does not terrorize.

---

## Background depth

Use a **3–5 layer multiplane** approach:

1. Sky plate  
2. Distant mountains/hills  
3. Midground trees/structures  
4. Acting stage (path/clearing)  
5. Foreground soft parallax (grass, leaves, rocks)

Depth cues: atmospheric perspective (cooler/fainter distance), overlapping shapes, and gentle parallax during camera moves.

---

## Visual comedy

Comedy is physical, kind, and readable.

### Tools
- Anticipation and soft impacts (Bobo specialty)
- Scale jokes (small Bobo / large Mama Tembo) without mean status humor
- Prop mishaps that reset quickly
- Expression takes held just long enough for kids to catch

### Avoid
- Cringe humiliation
- Gross-out
- Aggressive slapstick injury
- Rapid seizure-level flashing

---

## Emotional scenes

Emotional cinematography is quieter:

- Closer lenses / intimate framing
- Softer light and slower pushes
- More held faces; fewer cuts
- Supportive group staging (friends enter frame to comfort)
- Color slightly warmer and less contrasty

Lesson moments should feel like a hug, not a lecture slide.

---

## Premium checklist (every key frame)

1. Warm sunrise identity present  
2. Character silhouette clear  
3. Background depth layered  
4. Accent colors controlled  
5. Faces emotionally readable  
6. No generic AI-cartoon sameness  
7. Feels like Sunrise Valley and nowhere else  

---
